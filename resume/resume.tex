			{Daily calibration of Heston (1993) and Bates (1996) parameters to CBOE SPX intraday option trades (2012 onward) with QuantLib: one pooled, moneyness-normalised implied-vol surface per trading day, box-constrained Levenberg--Marquardt from multiple data-seeded starts, an implied-vol-space acceptance gate rejecting boundary-pegged fits, and Feller and cross-day stability diagnostics. 			{Feed-forward networks trained to approximate QuantLib Heston prices of barrier and Asian options on SPX, with training data generated from about 1,600 historical Heston calibrations (2012--2024); pricing errors below 1\% at up to 99.8\% lower computation time. Basis of the working paper below.}

			\resumeProject{Heston simulation}{\ghlink{heston-sim}}
